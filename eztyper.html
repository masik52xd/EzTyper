Everything you need.
Nothing you don’t.

A small, fast editor that stays out of your way while giving you the tools writers and coders actually use.

📑

Multi-tab interface

Work with multiple text documents at once and jump between them instantly.

🔢

Line numbering

A clear view of your text and code structure, always in sight.

🎨

Customizable look

Color themes like Nordic Dark, adjustable text margins in pixels, and your own font and size for the preview.

⏱️

Pomodoro timer

Built-in timer to keep your focus sharp and your work time under control.

💡

Idea of the Day

A tiny generator of inspiration for quick notes when the page feels too blank.

🧹

Text cleaner

Quickly clean up and format messy text with a single click.

📖

Reading time

Automatically estimates how long your text takes to read, aloud or silently.

👁️

Live preview

Press F5 to see your formatted text in real time.

🪶

Lightweight

More features than Notepad, yet it uses fewer resources. Runs well even on older machines.

Up and running in a minute

The release is a portable version, so there is nothing to install.

Download

Grab the portable build from the Releases page on GitHub.

Extract

Unpack it into any regular folder, such as Documents or a USB drive.

Start typing

Run EzTyper and enjoy. Your notes, your pace.

Heads up: do not place EzTyper in system folders like Program Files, Program Files (x86) or Windows.
